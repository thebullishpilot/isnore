Frequently asked questions

Why does it say "Counting your night" in the morning?
The part of iSnore that recognizes snoring can only run while the app is on screen. Overnight it saves the sounds that stand out, and when you tap Stop it checks them. Keep the app open until it finishes; it usually takes a few seconds.

Where should I put my iPhone?
On the nightstand, within arm's reach of your head, plugged in. Listening all night uses the battery.

Can I lock the screen?
Yes. iSnore keeps listening with the screen locked, and counts the night when you open the app in the morning. Don't swipe the app closed.

It missed quiet snores, or it counted other sounds.
Open Settings in the app and change Sensitivity. Use High if quiet snores are being missed, and Low if other sounds are being counted.

Why does it compare "snores an hour"?
So a short night and a long night compare fairly. Nights shorter than an hour are saved but aren't measured against the others.

How does it know what's working?
You choose what to try on the Tips tab. iSnore compares your nights with it against your nights without it. It needs at least two nights each way, and a few nights is still a small sample, so treat it as a hint.

A phone call or alarm interrupted the night.
iSnore pauses while something else uses the microphone and picks up again afterwards.

Can I use my Apple Watch instead?
Yes. Start the night from the iSnore app on the watch. It listens on the watch and sends the night to your iPhone when you stop; the iPhone does the counting, so open iSnore there to see the result. A long night can take a while to arrive. Charge the watch first: listening all night uses a lot of battery.

How do I delete my data?
Touch and hold a night on the Progress tab to delete it, or use Delete all nights in Settings. Deleting the app removes everything.

Is iSnore a medical device?
No. It counts sounds and cannot diagnose sleep apnea or any other condition. Loud snoring with pauses in breathing, gasping, or heavy daytime sleepiness are reasons to see a doctor.
