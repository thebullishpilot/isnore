Privacy Policy

iSnore · Effective October 12, 2026

In short: iSnore does not collect, store or share your personal data. The sound of your room is analyzed on your device and never leaves it. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Who we are

iSnore is published by Heart and Soil Living LTD. Questions about this policy: harmvadev@gmail.com.

The microphone

iSnore uses the microphone only while you have started a night of listening, and it stops when you tap Stop. The sound is analyzed on your iPhone or Apple Watch as it arrives, to recognize and count snores. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and the app does not transcribe or interpret speech.

Snore clips

If "Save snore clips" is on, the app keeps a ten-second recording of the loudest snore from each half hour so you can hear it in the morning. These recordings are stored only on your iPhone. You can turn the setting off, delete a night, or delete all nights at any time in the app.

Data stored on your device

Each night's snore count, the times of the snores, the things you chose to try, any saved clips and your settings are stored only on your device. If you count a night on your Apple Watch, the watch sends that night's results directly to the iSnore app on your own iPhone. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Data we collect

None. We do not operate servers for this app, and the app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code. If you email us for support, we use your email address and message only to reply to you.

Not a medical device

iSnore counts sounds. It does not diagnose, treat or monitor any medical condition.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
